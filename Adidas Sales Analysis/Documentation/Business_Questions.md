Business Questions 

The following business questions are explored in this Power BI project.



1. What is the total sales revenue?

2. What is the total profit?

3. What is the total number of units sold?

4. What is the average profit margin?

5. What is the average price per unit?

6. How do total sales vary by month?

7. How do total sales vary by state?

8. How do total sales vary by region?

9. Which products generate the highest sales?

10. Which retailers generate the highest sales?
